## Comparative Adjectives (-er / more / less): quick rule

En inglés hay dos formas principales de formar comparativos:

| Adjective length | Comparative form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 syllable | adjective + er | tall → taller |
| 2 syllables (some) | adjective + er | happy → happier |
| 3+ syllables | more / less + adjective | interesting → more interesting / less interesting |

Los **comparative adjectives** se usan para **comparar dos cosas**.

La estructura básica es:

```
comparative adjective + than
```

Ejemplos:

- This beat is **heavier than** the previous one.
- My verse is **better than** the last one.
- This song is **more emotional than** the first one.


## 1️⃣ Comparativos con **-er**

Los **[adjetivos cortos](./short-adjectives)** (normalmente de una sílaba) forman el comparativo añadiendo **-er**.

```
adjective + er + than
```

Ejemplos:

- fast → **faster than**
- strong → **stronger than**
- cold → **colder than**

Oraciones:

- This flow is **faster than** yours.
- My voice sounds **stronger than** before.

---

Los **[adjetivos largos](./long-adjectives)** (normalmente de **dos o más sílabas**) usan **more** o **less**.

## 2️⃣ Comparativos con **more**


```
more + adjective + than
```

Ejemplos:

- creative → **more creative than**
- powerful → **more powerful than**
- difficult → **more difficult than**

Oraciones:

- This rhyme is **more creative than** the previous one.
- That verse is **more emotional than** mine.


## 3️⃣ Comparativos con **less**

También podemos comparar usando **less** para indicar **menor grado**.

```
less + adjective + than
```

Ejemplos:

- This beat is **less aggressive than** the last one.
- My new verse is **less complicated than** the previous one.
- This problem is **less serious than** it looks.


### Después de **than**, en inglés informal normalmente usamos:

* **object pronoun** (me, him, her, us, them)

Ejemplo:

* ✔ She’s taller than **me**.
* ✔ He runs faster than **him** (aunque aquí normalmente diríamos *than he does*).

En inglés **formal o muy cuidadoso**, se usa el **subject pronoun + verb**.

Ejemplos:

- She is taller than **I am**.
- He runs faster than **he does**.


## 4️⃣ Reglas comunes de ortografía

Al formar comparativos con **-er**, a veces cambia la ortografía.

**1. Consonante duplicada**

Si el adjetivo es **corto (CVC)**, la consonante final se duplica.

- big → **bigger**
- hot → **hotter**
- sad → **sadder**

**2. Adjetivos terminados en -y**

La **y cambia a i**.

- happy → **happier**
- easy → **easier**
- heavy → **heavier**

**3. Algunos comparativos irregulares**

- good → **better**
- bad → **worse**
- far → **farther / further**

Ejemplos:

- This line is **better than** the previous one.
- That decision was **worse than** expected.


## 🚀 Cómo aprender a usar **comparative adjectives**

### ✅ 1. Memoriza la fórmula

```
comparative + than
```

Ejemplo mental rápido:

- stronger **than**
- better **than**
- more powerful **than**


### ✅ 2. Micro-drill (entrenamiento rápido)

Completa mentalmente usando **comparative adjectives**:

1. Today is ______ than yesterday.  
2. This beat is ______ than the previous one.  
3. My new verse is ______ than the last one.  
4. This problem is ______ than it looks.  
5. My English is ______ than last year.  
6. This road is ______ than the other one.  
7. That idea is ______ than mine.  


### ✅ 3. Conecta con tu vida (mejor retención)

Crea comparaciones reales sobre tu vida.

* ✔ My English is **better than last year**.  
* ✔ I write **stronger lyrics than before**.  
* ✔ My discipline is **stronger than my excuses**.


### ✅ 4. Haz contrastes

Practica **comparar dos cosas directamente**.

* ✔ Comparatives: *stronger than*  
* ✔ Superlatives: *the strongest*

Ejemplos:

* He is **stronger than** me  
* This verse is **better than** the last one  
* My new flow is **faster than** before  


### ✅ 5. Aplicado

#### 📝 Ejemplos estilo lírica

- ✔ My pain is **deeper than** your silence.  
- ✔ My voice is **louder than** their lies.  
- ✔ This truth is **heavier than** the beat.  
- ✔ My scars are **older than** your anger.  
- ✔ My story is **darker than** the night.  
- ✔ My mind is **stronger than** the pressure.  
- ✔ My faith is **bigger than** my fear.


## ⚠️ Errores típicos

- 🚫 **more stronger than**  
✔ **stronger than**

- 🚫 **more better than**  
✔ **better than**

- 🚫 **more easier than**  
✔ **easier than**

- 🚫 **more faster than**  
✔ **faster than**
