## How to express proportional relationships using 'the + comparative, the + comparative'.

## Parallel Change

**Parallel change** es una estructura comparativa que expresa una **relación proporcional entre dos cambios**.

En español suele equivaler a:

- *Cuanto más..., más...*
- *Cuanto menos..., menos...*

Sirve para mostrar que **un cambio provoca otro cambio al mismo tiempo**.

Ejemplo básico:

> **The harder you work, the better you become.**


## Estructura

La estructura sigue un patrón fijo.

### 1️⃣ Estructura básica

```
the + comparative..., the + comparative...
```

Ejemplo:

* The more you practice, the better you get.
> Cuanto más practicas, mejor te vuelves.


### 2️⃣ Con sujeto explícito

Cada parte puede tener su propio sujeto.

```
The + comparative + subject + verb, the + comparative + subject + verb
```

Ejemplo:

* The more I study English, the more confident I feel.
> Cuanto más estudio inglés, más seguro me siento.


### 3️⃣ Con comparativos largos (more / less)

También funciona con comparativos de dos o más sílabas.

```
The more + adjective..., the more + adjective...
```

Ejemplo:

* The more disciplined you are, the more successful you become.
> Cuanto más disciplinado eres, más exitoso te vuelves.

## 🚀 Cómo aprender a usar **parallel change**

### ✅ 1. Memoriza la fórmula

```
The + comparative..., the + comparative...
```

Idea clave:

**Dos comparativos conectados para mostrar relación directa.**


### ✅ 2. Micro-drill (entrenamiento rápido)

Completa mentalmente:

1. The more you practice, the ______ you become.
2. The harder you train, the ______ you get.
3. The more you read, the ______ your vocabulary becomes.
4. The less you worry, the ______ you feel.
5. The more consistent you are, the ______ the results.
6. The longer you wait, the ______ it becomes.
7. The more disciplined you are, the ______ your progress.


### ✅ 3. Conecta con tu vida (mejor retención)

Usa la estructura con situaciones reales.

* ✔ **The more I practice English, the more natural it feels.**
* ✔ **The more I write lyrics, the sharper my flow becomes.**
* ✔ **The more I study grammar, the clearer my ideas get.**


### ✅ 4. Haz contrastes

Comparar ayuda a fijar la estructura. Contrasta **parallel change** con otras estructuras básicas de comparación.

* ✔ **Comparative:** This verse is stronger than the last one.  
* ✔ **Superlative:** This is the strongest verse on the album.  
* ✔ **Equality:** My flow is as aggressive as yours.  
* ✔ **Difference:** This beat is not as heavy as the previous one.  
* ✔ **Cause & Effect:** The beat was so heavy that the crowd went crazy.  
* ✔ **Repeated comparative:** My flow is getting better and better.  
* ✔ **Parallel change:** The more I write, the better my lyrics become.


### ✅ 5. Aplicado

#### 📝 Ejemplos estilo lírica

- ✔ **The deeper the pain, the realer the verse.**
- ✔ **The darker the night, the brighter the vision.**
- ✔ **The more they doubt me, the harder I grind.**
- ✔ **The more pressure I feel, the sharper I write.**
- ✔ **The louder the beat, the stronger the message.**
- ✔ **The more I remember, the deeper the rhyme.**
- ✔ **The more truth I spit, the harder it hits.**


## ⚠️ Errores típicos

### Errores comunes

* ❌ *More you practice, better you get*  
✔ **The more you practice, the better you get**

* ❌ *The more you practice, better you get*  
✔ **The more you practice, the better you get**

* ❌ *The more practice you do, the more better you get*  
✔ **The more you practice, the better you get**

🚫 olvidar **the** en cada parte  
🚫 usar **more better / more stronger**  
🚫 romper el paralelismo entre las dos partes
