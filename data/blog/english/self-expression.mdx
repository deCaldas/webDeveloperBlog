Contar nuestra propia historia no requiere un vocabulario de nivel experto, sino **precisión y claridad**. Para ayudarte a construir tu narrativa personal, hemos seleccionado 21 estructuras esenciales que cubren desde tus pasiones hasta tus cambios de vida.

### **Identidad y Pasiones**

1. **I love ___ because it helps me ___.**
   * *Example:* I love **reading** because it helps me **de-stress after work**.
2. **I’ve always been interested in ___ because ___.**
   * *Example:* I’ve always been interested in **coding** because **I enjoy solving puzzles**.
3. **If I had to describe myself in one word, it would be ___ because ___.**
   * *Example:* It would be **resilient** because **I never give up on my goals**.
4. **One thing that defines me is my ability to ___.**
   * *Example:* One thing that defines me is my ability to **stay calm under pressure**.
5. **I feel most like myself when I am ___.**
   * *Example:* I feel most like myself when I am **hiking in the mountains**.

---

### **Hábitos y Transformación (The "Used To" Pattern)**

6. **I used to ___ but now I ___.**
   * *Example:* I used to **eat fast food** but now I **cook my own meals**.
7. **I didn’t use to ___, but now I ___.**
   * *Example:* I didn’t use to **wake up early**, but now I **exercise at 6 AM**.
8. **I’m not the same person I was when I ___.**
   * *Example:* I’m not the same person I was when I **started my first job**.
9. **Back then, I ___, but now I ___.**
   * *Example:* Back then, I **worried about everyone's opinion**, but now I **focus on my own happiness**.
10. **The biggest change in my life is that now I ___.**
    * *Example:* The biggest change in my life is that now I **live in a different country**.
11. **I have learned to value ___ more than I did before.**
    * *Example:* I have learned to value **my free time** more than I did before.

---

### **Lecciones y Crecimiento Personal**

12. **One thing I will never do again is ___ because ___.**
    * *Example:* One thing I will never do again is **work without a contract** because **it’s too risky**.
13. **It took me a long time to realize that ___.**
    * *Example:* It took me a long time to realize that **failure is part of learning**.
14. **The moment I knew everything had changed was when ___.**
    * *Example:* The moment I knew everything had changed was when **I moved out of my parents' house**.
15. **I am still working on my ability to ___.**
    * *Example:* I am still working on my ability to **say "no" without feeling guilty**.
16. **Looking back, I wish I had ___.**
    * *Example:* Looking back, I wish I had **traveled more in my early twenties**.

---

### **Estado de Ánimo y Perspectiva**

17. **Regarding ___, on good days I feel like ___, but on bad days I feel like ___.**
    * *Example:* Regarding **my career**, on good days I feel like **a success**, but on bad days I feel like **an imposter**.
18. **Lately, I’ve been feeling ___ about ___.**
    * *Example:* Lately, I’ve been feeling **optimistic** about **the future of my project**.
19. **What keeps me motivated is the fact that ___.**
    * *Example:* What keeps me motivated is the fact that **I am making a difference**.
20. **I try to remind myself that ___ whenever I feel overwhelmed.**
    * *Example:* I try to remind myself that **one step at a time is enough**.
21. **Currently, my main priority is to ___.**
    * *Example:* Currently, my main priority is to **improve my English fluency**.

---

### **Tip Pro: ¿Cómo usar estas frases?**
No intentes usarlas todas a la vez. Elige **tres frases** que resuenen con tu situación actual y escribe un pequeño párrafo combinándolas. La clave de una buena narrativa es la **honestidad** y la **estructura**.
